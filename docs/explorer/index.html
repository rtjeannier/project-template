The simulation

Each simulated season plays out weeks 9–17 from today's real rosters and FAAB: waivers (every team bids what a player is worth to it, with random noise so bids aren't predictable), games with injuries and weekly luck, and the weekly cut, until one team is left.

What has to go right

The conditions that move a team's title odds the most: who gets cut and when, key injuries, and key pickups. Each is judged only on the seasons where it could matter (for example, a pickup only counts seasons where the player hit waivers while the team was alive), so simply surviving long isn't credited to it. Tick any combination to see how often it happens and the title odds when it does; the Seasons list below then shows only seasons where all ticked conditions happened. These are associations, not guarantees.
