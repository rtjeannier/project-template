The simulation

Every team bids its fair bid (the rational bid at that moment) times a random multiplier drawn from a normal distribution around 1× (95th percentile 2×; a draw at or below 0 is a $0 bid, since bidding is free). Each bid is drawn independently, so comparing outcomes across multipliers is a fair experiment: if bids at 1.5× go with more league wins than bids at 1×, overbidding caused it.

Paths to victory

Pick a team to see how its winning seasons differ from its losing ones, week by week. Everything compares the team with itself, so its built-in strength drops out. These are associations: getting a player or saving FAAB isn't randomized the way bid multipliers are, so a strong position can drive both.
